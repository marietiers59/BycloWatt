Our services

Design

At BycloWatt, we design entertainment equipment powered by human energy. Our team's abilities have been forged by years of practical development of artistic and technical skills. Indeed, we are so passionate about these fields that we started training at home, in our free time, as early as secondary school! Most of us have spent time drawing, then designing and conceptualising various items.

Every project is conceived to be self-running, sustainable and interactive, with the help of mechanical, piezoelectric and electrogenerative technologies. Dancing, cycling or moving becomes a way to produce energy: the public is the heart of the system.

Our aim is plain: creating unique and educational experiences which demonstrate that it is possible to have fun without overconsuming electricity.

Rental

We rent out our energy-independent attractions for all types of events: festivals, cultural events, student events, and public or private functions.

Our equipment is designed to be easy to use, safe and completely independent of the electrical grid. By renting BycloWatt products, organisers reduce their carbon footprint while offering the public an immersive, participatory and original experience.

Mediation

BycloWatt also offers on-site interventions to complement events with fun and educational environmental awareness.

Our teams run the attractions, explain how they work, and raise public awareness about renewable energy and energy conservation. Through hands-on experience, we show that everyone can play a part in the ecological transition.

Our activities turn festive occasions into positive educational experiences, accessible to all audiences.
